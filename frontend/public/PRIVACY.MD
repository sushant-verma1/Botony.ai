# Privacy Policy — Medical AI Prototype

**Last Updated: June 2026**

## 1. Data We Collect
- Account information: first name, last name, email, age
- Conversation data: messages you send and AI responses received
- Authentication data: securely hashed passwords, session tokens

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- To provide and maintain the chat service
- To detect emergency situations and respond appropriately
- To improve the quality and safety of AI responses
- To maintain audit logs for safety and compliance review

## 3. Data Security
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text
- Refresh tokens are stored in HttpOnly cookies, inaccessible to
  client-side scripts
- Access tokens are kept in memory only and are never persisted to disk

## 4. Data Retention
Free-tier conversation data is automatically deleted after 30 days.

## 5. Your Rights
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal
data at any time by contacting the application administrator.

## 6. Third-Party Services
Conversation content is processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate
responses. Refer to Anthropic's privacy policy for details on how they
handle data submitted via their API.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will be
communicated to users.
